# Authorization

Every engagement needs a written scope.

## Before testing

1. Get the scope in writing: targets, time window, allowed techniques.
2. Record it against the engagement so the console shows it.
3. Confirm the rules of engagement with the client contact.

## During testing

- Stay inside the scope even when a path outside it looks easier.
- Prefer passive collection over intrusive actions.
- Stop and ask when you find something that changes the risk profile.

## After testing

- Deliver findings with reproduction steps and remediation.
- Remove the access you created.
- Keep the evidence you are legally allowed to keep.
